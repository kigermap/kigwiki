# Chronicle Overview

The chronicle is the archive's time axis. A dated entry should identify the event, place, source, public-access boundary, confidence level, and unresolved questions. Monthly digests hold developing information; annual volumes contain only stable, de-duplicated records.

## Published annual volumes

- [2021 Kigurumi Chronicle](../years/2021/index.md)
- 2022–2025 are available in the default-language archive and in the Japanese, Russian, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese editions.

## Current collection

- [July 2026 public-source digest](../digests/2026-07/index.md)

Unverified allegations, private conversations, unauthorized photographs, sensitive addresses, and claims without traceable dates are not published as facts.
