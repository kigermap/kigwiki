# 2010 Kigurumi Chronicle

> The 2009 volume has now been added. This correction moves shared rules to the earlier evidence point and retains only material 2010 changes; specialist gatherings without reliable dates, places, and outcomes are not reconstructed from later evidence.

## 1. Evidence boundary

Tokyo Game Show 2010's official overview lists September 16-17 as business days and September 18-19 as public days at Makuhari Messe. This volume uses the organizer's program page, participation rules, Cosplay Dance Night page, and September 19 closing report to distinguish announced programs, published rules, and the event as confirmed after closing.[^tgs-overview][^tgs-cosplay][^tgs-rules][^tgs-night][^tgs-report]

Tokyo Game Show is a general game exhibition, not an animegao kigurumi event. Its rules directly mention “large kigurumi,” translated by the organizer as “large size full-body suits,” but identify no animegao participant, count, or attendance outcome. This volume records **the rule boundary for large kigurumi at a public exhibition and the adjacent cosplay infrastructure that was programmed**. It does not turn a general cosplay program or total exhibition attendance into evidence of kigurumi attendance.

## 2. Verifiable nodes

### September 18-19: TGS added an integrated set of cosplay programs

The public-day page designated Yasuragi Mall between Halls 3 and 4 as the cosplay area and announced a Cosplay Grand Prix, invited overseas cosplayers, Coswall photography, performance slots, and Cosplay Dance Night. The September 19 closing report explicitly calls these “newly planned cosplay events starting this year,” confirming that the program belonged to the completed TGS 2010 event rather than to later recollection or secondary retelling.[^tgs-cosplay][^tgs-report]

The closing report does not provide participant lists for every program and does not identify animegao or large-kigurumi attendance. The stable node is therefore that the organizer added and delivered the integrated cosplay initiative, not that a particular subgroup attended.

### September 18-19: Hall 7 changing, cloakroom, and early access formed one workflow

The organizer placed the cosplay changing room in Hall 7 and scheduled early opening from the first train at Kaihin-Makuhari Station until 8:00; participants who finished before 8:00 had to rejoin the end of the general-admission queue. The JPY 500 fee, designated changing, and costume checks trace to the [2007 volume](../2007/index.md), while sewing kits are retained in the [2009 volume](../2009/index.md); the common workflow is not repeated here.[^tgs-cosplay][^tgs-rules]

Large-kigurumi, photo-consent, wall-background, and non-commercial-use boundaries were already public in 2007. The 2010 archive shows no material change, so they remain continuity rather than a new node.[^tgs-rules]

### September 18: Dance Night extended changing-room access to 21:00

Cosplay Dance Night was listed for 18:00-20:00 on the Hall 8 event stage. The archived page marks it as finished and states that only Dance Night participants could continue using the Hall 7 changing room until 21:00 that day.[^tgs-night]

This establishes an infrastructure extension for a general evening cosplay program. The page specifies no animegao or kigurumi act, so it cannot establish attendance by either group.

## 5. Annual conclusion

The stable 2010 record is Tokyo Game Show's new integrated cosplay initiative, Hall 7 early changing, and the Dance Night extension to 21:00. The base boundaries for large kigurumi, changing and cloakroom use, photo consent, wall backgrounds, and non-commercial distribution remain in 2007; 2009 keeps later operational additions. The closing report confirms the new initiative within the completed TGS 2010 event, but does not prove attendance by animegao or large-kigurumi participants.
