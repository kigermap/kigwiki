# 2002 Kigurumi Chronicle

> This volume records events, works and publications from this year. Announcements and completed events are identified separately.

## Verifiable nodes

### September 20-22: a neighboring public convention publishes its calendar and venue

The official outline makes September 20 a business day restricted to press and invited visitors, makes September 21-22 public days running 10:00-17:00, and identifies Makuhari Messe, organizer CESA, and co-organizer Nikkei BP.[^tgs-outline] This is verifiable annual context, not a kigurumi participation record.
