# 2002 Kigurumi Chronicle

> The 2003-2025 volumes are complete. Following the rule to avoid completed years and move backward by only one year per run, this volume adds 2002 only. Surviving first-party pages establish the calendar and venue of a neighboring convention that later published large-kigurumi rules, but they do not establish specialist kigurumi / animegao rules or participation in 2002. This volume records that evidence boundary instead of backfilling it from 2003 or 2004.

## 1. Evidence boundary

The Nikkei BP / CESA first-party archive for Tokyo Game Show 2002 lists September 20 as a business day, September 21-22 as public days, and the Japan Convention Center (Makuhari Messe) as the venue. It explicitly labels 150,000 as planned attendance.[^tgs-outline] These fields establish neighboring public-convention context only. Planned attendance is not a final result, and a planned calendar does not prove that every activity took place.

The accessible 2002 archive reviewed here consists of the Business Person Side homepage and event outline. It supplies no directly citable kigurumi, animegao, large-kigurumi, changing, cloakroom, or photography rules. Its link to the event's former official root now leads to a later site, so the current page cannot reconstruct the 2002 visitor rules. Inside Doll's first-party pages place that site's own start in April 2003. This constrains backdating but does not prove that no other specialist site or activity existed in 2002.[^inside-main][^inside-news]

## 2. Verifiable nodes

### September 20-22: a neighboring public convention publishes its calendar and venue

The official outline makes September 20 a business day restricted to press and invited visitors, makes September 21-22 public days running 10:00-17:00, and identifies Makuhari Messe, organizer CESA, and co-organizer Nikkei BP.[^tgs-outline] This is verifiable annual context, not a kigurumi participation record.

### Surviving material does not form a citable kigurumi rule chain

The [2004 volume](../2004/index.md) can directly cite “large kigurumi,” designated changing, cloakroom service, costume inspection, and photography consent. The accessible 2002 pages provide only event-outline and business information. Their silence neither proves that no related costume appeared nor makes the 2004 rules an existing 2002 policy.

### The 2003 specialist-site start is not moved backward

Inside Doll's main page says “2003/04/23 start,” while its update log places formal opening on April 5, 2003. Despite their date conflict, both locate the site's public start in 2003.[^inside-main][^inside-news] Its specialist online-publishing node therefore remains in the [2003 volume](../2003/index.md), not 2002.

## 5. Annual conclusion

The stable 2002 record is limited to Tokyo Game Show's announced calendar, public days, and Makuhari venue, plus the boundary that these materials do not establish specialist kigurumi / animegao rules or participation. The 2003 specialist website and 2004 public large-kigurumi rules remain in their source years. Publishing this gap is more accurate than constructing a complete story from later evidence. The next unfinished year is 2001.
