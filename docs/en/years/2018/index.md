# 2018 Kigurumi Chronicle

> This volume records events, works and publications from this year. Announcements and completed events are identified separately.

## Verified chronology

### March 24-25: AnimeJapan 2018 extends public-space rules for kigurumi

AnimeJapan 2018's public days ran March 24-25 in Tokyo Big Sight East Halls 1-8. Cosplayers World used East Halls 3 and 8 and provided paid cosplay registration, changing rooms, cloakrooms, indoor and outdoor photography areas, early changing, and a parade.[^aj-outline][^aj-cosplay]

The page continued the consent, pose, changing-room, and cloakroom provisions. This volume does not count those continuing provisions as new 2018 rules; the year's unique governance node is the larger venue footprint and explicit parade-attendant responsibility.

### Academic year 2018: animegao mask production enters graduate research

Keio University's repository records Haruka Jibiki's master's thesis, “Construction of a matching service for animegao-kigurumi mask production: through the activities of mask order-to-sales business ‘Kigurumi-Ya,’” advised by Kazunori Sugiura and published by the Graduate School of Media Design. The record describes x + 65 pages and classifies it as Media Design thesis no. 689 for academic year 2018.[^keio][^ndl]

The title itself establishes mask production, maker matching, and Kigurumi-Ya's order-sales activity as the research subject. Because the repository supplies no abstract, this archive does not infer methods, sample size, or outcomes. The durable claim is narrower: production supply, commissioning, and service design had become a formal master's research topic.
