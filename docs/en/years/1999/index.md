# 1999 Kigurumi Chronicle

> The 2000-2025 volumes are complete. Following the rule to avoid completed years and move backward only one year per run, this volume adds 1999 alone. The official Tokyo Game Show thirtieth-anniversary archive confirms the dates, attendance, and a spring stage program for the spring and autumn editions, but does not label any photograph, program, or statistic as kigurumi / animegao. This volume records that adjacent public context and its evidence boundary without backfilling from later years.

## 1. Evidence boundary

The official Tokyo Game Show history identifies the spring edition as the sixth show, held March 19-21 with 163,448 visitors, and records a CESA quiz championship on the event stage. The seventh, autumn edition ran September 17-19 with 163,866 visitors.[^tgs-history] These retrospective organizer records are sufficient to confirm that the shows and the listed stage program occurred.

The same archive supplies photographs and posters for both editions, but gives no item-level identification of people, costume categories, or kigurumi / animegao participation. Attendance totals, a general stage program, and uncatalogued photographs cannot independently establish specialist rules, community participation, or a particular performer. The page also provides no clear permission for this site to reproduce the images, so this volume links the source without copying them.

Inside Doll's first-party home page and update log both place that specialist site's public start in 2003, although the precise date conflicts between the pages.[^inside-main][^inside-news] Those sources prevent the site from being backdated to 1999; they do not prove that no other kigurumi practice, personal page, or event existed that year.

## 2. Verifiable nodes

### March 19-21: TGS 1999 spring completion record

The official anniversary archive labels the sixth edition “1999 spring” and records March 19-21, 163,448 visitors, and the CESA quiz championship.[^tgs-history] This is adjacent public-event and program context, not a kigurumi participation record.

### September 17-19: TGS 1999 autumn completion record

The same page labels the seventh edition “1999 autumn” and records September 17-19 with 163,866 visitors.[^tgs-history] That total describes the show as a whole, not the scale, debut, or completion status of a costume category.

### Later specialist publishing and rules are not backdated

The [2003 volume](../2003/index.md) retains Inside Doll's public starting point, while the [2004 volume](../2004/index.md) retains the earliest currently verifiable public rules for large kigurumi. Neither is moved into 1999, and unidentified people in official archive photographs are not assigned to a performance category.

## 5. Annual conclusion

The stable record for 1999 is limited to the actual dates, organizer-retrospective attendance figures, and spring stage program for the two Tokyo Game Show editions, together with the fact that these materials do not prove specialist kigurumi / animegao rules or participation. The 2003 specialist website and 2004 public rules remain in their source years. The next unfinished year is 1998.
