# 2015 Kigurumi Chronicle

> This volume records events, works and publications from this year. Announcements and completed events are identified separately.

## Verified nodes

### March 21-22: AnimeJapan 2015 expands public cosplay space usable by kigurumi

AnimeJapan 2015's public days ran March 21-22 in Tokyo Big Sight East Halls 1-6. Cosplayers World provided indoor and outdoor cosplay areas, paid registration, changing rooms, cloakroom service, and official anime photography sets. The organizer described the changing room and outdoor area as expanded from the previous year.[^aj-outline][^aj-cosplay]

Daily cosplay registration cost JPY 500. Changing rooms ran from 10:00 to 17:00, with an early 7:00-9:00 session. Cloakroom service cost a separate JPY 500 per day, allowed repeated access, and ran until 16:30; the indoor cosplay area closed at 16:30 and the outdoor area at 16:00.[^aj-cosplay] For participants transporting a mask, costume, and accessories, these hours, fees, and access conditions determined whether public participation was practical.

The rules confined large constructions, kigurumi, and doll costumes to the indoor and outdoor cosplay areas. Participants wearing costumes that limited vision or movement were asked to remove or carry obstructive parts or move with an assistant so as not to impede surrounding visitors. Photography was limited to designated areas, required the subject's permission, and could not involve forced poses; cameras and phones could not be used for photography in changing rooms.[^aj-cosplay] The text directly addresses vision, movement, privacy, and consent in a kigurumi-relevant setting.

### After closing: the organizer report confirms the expanded areas and photography facilities operated

The post-event report confirms AnimeJapan 2015 ran March 20-22 and drew more than 120,000 visitors overall. That figure covers the business area, public area, and the entire event and cannot be converted into a kigurumi count. The report also says Cosplayers World's changing room and outdoor area were larger than in the previous edition, more cosplayers gathered than the year before, and queues formed for official anime sets and general-purpose photography backdrops.[^aj-report]

The report distinguishes facilities that actually operated from plans published before the event. It still does not establish a specialist kigurumi gathering, a particular animegao character, or the number of kigurumi participants, so this remains a record of public-event infrastructure.
