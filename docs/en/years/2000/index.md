# 2000 Kigurumi Chronicle

> The 2001-2025 volumes are complete. Following the rule to avoid completed years and move backward only one year per run, this volume adds 2000 alone. The official Tokyo Game Show thirtieth-anniversary archive confirms the dates and attendance of the spring and autumn editions, plus the autumn milestone of one million cumulative visitors, but does not label any photograph, program, or statistic as kigurumi / animegao. This volume records that adjacent public context and its evidence boundary without backfilling from later years.

## 1. Evidence boundary

The official Tokyo Game Show history identifies the spring edition as the eighth show, held March 31-April 2 with 131,708 visitors. The ninth, autumn edition ran September 22-24 with 137,400 visitors; the archive also records that cumulative attendance reached one million and that a commemorative ceremony was held.[^tgs-history] These retrospective organizer records are sufficient to confirm that the shows themselves occurred.

The same archive supplies photographs and posters for both editions, but gives no item-level identification of people, costume categories, or kigurumi / animegao participation. Attendance totals, the cumulative milestone, and uncatalogued photographs cannot independently establish specialist rules, community participation, or a particular performer. The page also provides no clear permission for this site to reproduce the images, so this volume links the source without copying them.

Inside Doll's first-party home page places that specialist site's start in April 2003. Its update log also places the public opening in 2003, although the precise day conflicts with the home page.[^inside-main][^inside-news] Those sources prevent the site from being backdated to 2000; they do not prove that no other kigurumi practice, personal page, or event existed that year.

## 2. Verifiable nodes

### March 31-April 2: TGS 2000 spring completion record

The official anniversary archive labels the eighth edition “2000 spring” and records March 31-April 2 with 131,708 visitors.[^tgs-history] This is adjacent public-event context, not a kigurumi participation record.

### September 22-24: TGS 2000 autumn completion record

The same page labels the ninth edition “2000 autumn,” records September 22-24 and 137,400 visitors, and notes the one-million cumulative attendance milestone and ceremony.[^tgs-history] That milestone describes the show as a whole, not the scale, debut, or completion status of a costume category.

### Later specialist publishing and rules are not backdated

The [2003 volume](../2003/index.md) retains Inside Doll's public starting point, while the [2004 volume](../2004/index.md) retains the earliest currently verifiable public rules for large kigurumi. Neither is moved into 2000, and unidentified people in official archive photographs are not assigned to a performance category.

## 5. Annual conclusion

The stable record for 2000 is limited to the actual dates, organizer-retrospective attendance figures, and autumn cumulative milestone for the two Tokyo Game Show editions, together with the fact that these materials do not prove specialist kigurumi / animegao rules or participation. The 2003 specialist website and 2004 public rules remain in their source years. The next unfinished year is 1999.
