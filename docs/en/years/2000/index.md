# 2000 Kigurumi Chronicle

> This volume records events, works and publications from this year. Announcements and completed events are identified separately.

## Verifiable nodes

### March 31-April 2: TGS 2000 spring completion record

The official anniversary archive labels the eighth edition “2000 spring” and records March 31-April 2 with 131,708 visitors.[^tgs-history] This is adjacent public-event context, not a kigurumi participation record.

### September 22-24: TGS 2000 autumn completion record

The same page labels the ninth edition “2000 autumn,” records September 22-24 and 137,400 visitors, and notes the one-million cumulative attendance milestone and ceremony.[^tgs-history] That milestone describes the show as a whole, not the scale, debut, or completion status of a costume category.

## Sources

[^tgs-history]: Tokyo Game Show / CESA, “東京ゲームショウ30周年,” 2000 spring and autumn entries, <https://tgs.cesa.or.jp/2026/30th>
