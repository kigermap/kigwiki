# 2008 Kigurumi Chronicle

> The 2009-2025 volumes are complete. Following the rule to prepare only one earlier, unfinished year per run, this volume adds 2008. Surviving first-party pages verify the dates, venue, and closing result of Tokyo Game Show 2008, but do not provide directly verifiable evidence of animegao or large-kigurumi participation. This volume records both what the archive establishes and what it does not.

## 1. Evidence boundary

The official overview lists October 9-10 as business days and October 11-12 as public days at Makuhari Messe. The organizer's October 12 report confirms that the event closed. [^tgs-overview][^tgs-report]

Tokyo Game Show was a general games exhibition, not a specialist animegao kigurumi event. The currently accessible visitor archive exposes the overview, floor map, exhibitor list, ticket information, and access information; those pages do not directly describe animegao, bishoujo kigurumi, large kigurumi, changing, or photography workflows. [^tgs-overview][^tgs-map] Missing pages do not prove that no relevant participant attended, and the 2009 rules cannot be projected backward into 2008.

## 2. Verifiable nodes

### October 9-12: Tokyo Game Show 2008 took place

The overview places the exhibition at Makuhari Messe and opens October 11-12 to the public. The closing report gives a four-day attendance of 194,288, including 71,639 and 71,166 people on the two public days. [^tgs-overview][^tgs-report]

These figures establish only the scale of the general exhibition. They are not counts of kigurumi, animegao, or cosplay participation. This archive does not estimate a specialist community from total attendance or automatically classify display characters on a hall map as enthusiast kigurumi activity.

### The public visitor archive does not yet form a citable kigurumi rules chain

The 2008 visitor navigation and floor map document public exhibition space, but they do not provide citable instructions for cosplay, changing rooms, cloakrooms, photo consent, or large kigurumi. [^tgs-overview][^tgs-map]

By contrast, the official 2004 rules already name "large kigurumi," designated changing, cloakroom use, and photography permission; 2007 confirms continuity, and 2009 adds a published baggage-screening plan. [^tgs-2004-rules][^tgs-2009-rules] The 2008 volume is therefore retained as an archive gap between the early rules and later additions, without reconstructing its policy from either side.

## 5. Annual conclusion

The stable 2008 record is that Tokyo Game Show, an adjacent public setting, took place. The surviving official visitor archive does not establish animegao or large-kigurumi participation and cannot reconstruct specialist rules. Preserving that gap is more reliable than using 2009 evidence to manufacture a complete-looking 2008 narrative.
