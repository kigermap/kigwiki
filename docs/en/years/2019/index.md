# 2019 Kigurumi Chronicle

> This volume records events, works and publications from this year. Announcements and completed events are identified separately.

## Verified chronology

### March 23–24: FGO lists kigurumi / official cosplayer appearances at AnimeJapan 2019

Fate/Grand Order's official notice confirms AnimeJapan 2019 at Tokyo Big Sight on March 23–24 and places the FGO booth in East Hall 6, J73. Its March 11 update explicitly lists “kigurumi / official cosplayer appearances” as booth programming.[^fgo-aj]

This is direct evidence of an official kigurumi node. The source does not label it a specialist animegao event, so this archive does not enlarge the claim; it records an official character-kigurumi presentation in a public anime convention.

### The same event: Cosplayers World as adjacent public infrastructure

AnimeJapan's Cosplayers World page confirms the event at Tokyo Big Sight East Halls 1–8 and its cosplay participation arrangements.[^aj-cosplay] It does not establish that all participants were kigurumi performers. It is retained as infrastructure evidence for the public access, changing, carrying, and photography conditions on which full-head practice can depend.
