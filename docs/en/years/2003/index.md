# 2003 Kigurumi Chronicle

> The 2004-2025 volumes are complete. Following the rule to avoid completed years and move backward by only one year per run, this volume adds 2003 only. Surviving first-party pages confirm that a specialist website centered on bishoujo kigurumi was publicly active in April 2003, but two pages conflict on its start date. This volume preserves that conflict and does not turn fiction, page updates, or traffic into evidence of real community activity.

## 1. Evidence boundary

The surviving Inside Doll pages describe the site's main content as adult fantasy fiction centered on bishoujo kigurumi. They explicitly tell readers not to confuse fiction with real kigurumi performance or attempt potentially dangerous scenes.[^inside-main][^inside-entry] This volume records only the site's public existence, editorial scope, and update cadence. It does not reproduce adult stories, treat story titles as real events or craft records, or copy images without a clear license.

The main-page header says “2003/04/23 start,” while the update log dates “formal public opening” to April 5 and then lists updates on April 6, 12, and 20.[^inside-main][^inside-news] Both first-party statements cannot establish one exact date. The supported conclusion is limited to “publicly active in April 2003.”

## 2. Verifiable nodes

### April: a specialist site enters its public update period

The log labels April 5 as the formal opening and then records link additions and early submissions; the main page instead labels April 23 as the start.[^inside-main][^inside-news] This establishes a public space for bishoujo-kigurumi fantasy writing during the month, not a physical gathering, mask build, or performance on either date.

### May-June: editorial and submitted work appears continuously

The log continues with original and submitted writing, columns, and link updates in May and June; June entries directly include story titles containing “kigurumi.”[^inside-news] These notices show that a specialist writing and submission mechanism was operating. They remain evidence of early online publishing culture, not of story events having occurred in reality.

### July-December: a visible update cadence continues

From July 5 through December 20, the log continues to record serials, submissions, columns, and link updates. Including April, the surviving 2003 log contains 27 dated notices.[^inside-news] That count measures page-record density only; it is not a count of authors, readers, kigers, events, or works.

## 5. Annual conclusion

The stable 2003 record is that, by April, a Japanese specialist website explicitly centered on bishoujo kigurumi was publicly active and continued to publish editorial and submission notices through the year. This demonstrates visible early specialist online culture, not real performance, workshop production, offline gatherings, or community scale. The conflicting start dates remain unresolved.
