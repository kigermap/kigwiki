# 2005 Kigurumi Chronicle

> The 2004 and 2006-2025 volumes are complete. The surviving organizer archive confirms Tokyo Game Show 2005's planned dates and venue, but supplies no directly citable kigurumi / cosplay rules, actual attendance record, or closing result. This volume therefore records the evidence boundary instead of filling it with material from 2004 or 2006.

## 1. Research boundary

The organizer homepage lists September 16-18 and the Japan Convention Center (Makuhari Messe). A May 16 release separates September 16 as a business day from September 17-18 as public days and gives **targets** of 150,000 visitors, 130 exhibitors, and 1,500 booths.[^tgs-home][^tgs-release]

These are exhibition and outline pages for a general game show, not records of an animegao kigurumi event. Targets are not final attendance, and a planned calendar does not by itself establish that every program was completed. The accessible archive does not directly mention large kigurumi, mask-type cosplay, designated changing, photography consent, or actual participation. Adjacent concepts, exhibitor characters, and adjacent-year rules are therefore not inserted into 2005.

## 2. Verifiable nodes

### May 16: organizer announced the theme, calendar, and targets

The release announced the theme “最前列で、未来を見よう。” and listed the business day, two public days, Makuhari venue, admission prices, and target scale.[^tgs-release] This establishes only the planned public-show context; it contains no specialist kigurumi provision or participant record.

### September 16-18: official planned window; completion unresolved

The organizer homepage repeats September 16-18 and the Makuhari venue.[^tgs-home] No verifiable post-event summary was recovered in this review, so the volume records an **official planned window**, not “fully completed.” There is likewise no evidence that animegao or large kigurumi attended.

## 5. Annual conclusion

What can be stably recorded for 2005 is the organizer's planned Tokyo Game Show calendar, public days, and Makuhari venue. The accessible archive does not establish kigurumi / animegao participation, specialist rules, or event completion. Making that gap explicit is more accurate than copying the 2004 or 2006 rules. The next unfinished year is 2003.
