# 1997 Kigurumi Chronicle

> The 1998-2025 volumes are complete. Under the rule to avoid finished years and move backward by only one year per run, this volume adds only 1997. Tokyo Game Show's official thirtieth-anniversary history confirms the dates, total attendance, and move from one annual event to spring and autumn editions in 1997, but does not label any 1997 photograph, program, or statistic as kigurumi / animegao. This volume records contemporary public context and the evidence boundary without backfilling later facts.

## 1. Evidence boundary

The official history identifies the second edition as spring 1997, held April 4-6 with 121,172 visitors, and says that the response to the first edition led to spring and autumn events. It identifies the third edition as autumn 1997, held September 5-7 with 140,630 visitors.[^tgs-history] These fields establish both events and the change in event frequency.

The same page supplies photographs and posters, but its text does not identify the people, costume categories, or kigurumi / animegao status in individual images; the image alt text is also empty. It gives this archive no explicit reuse permission. This volume therefore links the first-party page without assigning unlabeled images to a specialist practice or copying the media.

The [1998 volume](../1998/index.md) and later volumes already retain adjacent event context, the 2003 specialist-site starting point, and 2004 public rules. This volume does not move those later nodes into 1997. The relevant first-party legacy site did not respond during this review; that access limitation proves neither the presence nor absence of other practices, personal pages, or events in 1997.

## 2. Verifiable nodes

### April 4-6: completed TGS 1997 spring record

The official retrospective labels the second edition “1997 spring,” gives April 4-6 and 121,172 visitors, and says the response to the first edition prompted spring and autumn events.[^tgs-history] This is event-wide operating context, not a record of kigurumi participation, making, or etiquette rules.

### September 5-7: completed TGS 1997 autumn record

The same source labels the third edition “1997 autumn” and gives September 5-7 and 140,630 visitors.[^tgs-history] Total attendance does not establish the scale, first appearance, or participation of any performance-costume category.

### Later specialist publishing and rules remain in their source years

The [2003 volume](../2003/index.md) retains the currently documented start of specialist web publishing, while the [2004 volume](../2004/index.md) retains the earliest currently verifiable public rules for large kigurumi. Neither is moved into 1997, and unlabeled people in official history images are not assigned an identity.

The stable 1997 record is limited to the actual dates of two Tokyo Game Shows, organizer-retrospective attendance, the move to two annual editions, and the fact that these materials do not prove specialist kigurumi / animegao rules or participation. Later specialist publishing and public rules remain in their source years. The next unfinished year is 1996.
