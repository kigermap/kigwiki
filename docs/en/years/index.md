# Years

Annual pages turn verified event records into stable volumes. Each volume keeps chronology, source quality, domain classification, cross-year de-duplication, and unresolved research leads visible.

## English edition

- [2020 Kigurumi Chronicle](2020/index.md)
- [2021 Kigurumi Chronicle](2021/index.md)
- [2022 Kigurumi Chronicle](2022/index.md)

The 2023–2025 annual volumes predate the full English edition. They remain available through the site's other language versions rather than being presented as complete English translations.

## Publication rule

Only one earlier year is prepared in each collection run. A completed year is not rebuilt unless a source supplies a material correction or update.
