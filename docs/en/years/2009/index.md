# 2009 Kigurumi Chronicle

> After the official 2006 rules were added, this volume no longer presents 2009 as the first published point for large kigurumi, changing, cloakroom use, and photo consent. It keeps only the 2009 baggage-screening plan, on-site sewing kits, registration cutoff, and closing confirmation that are new or independently verifiable.

## 1. Evidence boundary

Tokyo Game Show's official overview lists September 24-25 as business days and September 26-27 as public days at Makuhari Messe. This volume uses the organizer's overview, visitor rules, and September 27 closing report to distinguish rules announced in advance from the exhibition confirmed after closing.[^tgs-overview][^tgs-rules][^tgs-report]

This was a general game exhibition, not a specialist animegao kigurumi event. The rules still name “large kigurumi,” but identify no animegao participant, count, program, or attendance result. Costume, changing, storage, and photography terms already documented for 2006 are not repeated line by line.

## 2. Verifiable nodes

### September 26-27: the public-day baggage-screening plan included cosplay participants

The organizer said it would conduct baggage screening on both public days and warned cosplay participants carrying more belongings to allow additional entry time. Prohibited items included cosplay umbrellas, canes, rackets, and similar props longer than 50 centimetres, as well as sharp or dangerous objects that could injure others.[^tgs-rules]

This places cosplay luggage inside the year's published safety plan. It is not a screening-completion report and does not show that an animegao or large-kigurumi participant was actually screened.

### September 26-27: common rules continued; sewing kits and a cutoff were added

The 2009 rules continued the restriction on obstructive “large kigurumi” and the JPY 500 designated changing and cloakroom workflow, exit costume inspection, designated photo area, subject permission, and personal-site publication consent. Those common terms now sit in the [2006 volume](../2006/index.md); continuity is not presented here as a new system.[^tgs-rules]

The operational details retained for 2009 are that sewing kits were available from staff and changing-room registration had to be completed by 16:00.[^tgs-rules] They concern emergency maintenance and time management, not evidence of a workshop or making class.

### September 27: the closing report confirmed the exhibition as a whole

The organizer's report confirms that Tokyo Game Show 2009 ran from September 24 through 27 and closed, but does not separately report cosplay programs or large-kigurumi or animegao participation.[^tgs-report]
