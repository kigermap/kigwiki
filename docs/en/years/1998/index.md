# 1998 Kigurumi Chronicle

> The 1999-2025 volumes are complete. Under the rule to avoid finished years and move backward by only one year per run, this volume adds only 1998. Tokyo Game Show's official thirtieth-anniversary history confirms the dates, total attendance, and a spring queue gift for its two 1998 editions, but does not label any 1998 image, program, or statistic as kigurumi / animegao. This volume records contemporary public context and the evidence boundary without backfilling later facts.

## 1. Evidence boundary

The official history identifies the fourth edition as spring 1998, held March 20-22 with 147,913 visitors, and says commemorative gifts were given to the groups first in the admission queue. It identifies the fifth edition as autumn 1998, held October 9-11 with 156,455 visitors.[^tgs-history] These fields establish that both events and the stated spring queue arrangement occurred.

The same page supplies photographs and posters, but its 1998 entries do not identify depicted people, costume categories, or kigurumi / animegao status. It also gives this archive no explicit reuse permission. This volume links the first-party page without assigning unlabeled images to a specialist practice or copying the media.

Inside Doll's first-party homepage and update log both place that specialist site's public opening in 2003, although they disagree on the precise date.[^inside-main][^inside-news] They prevent that site from being moved into 1998, but do not prove that no other practice, personal page, or event existed that year.

## 2. Verifiable nodes

### March 20-22: completed TGS 1998 spring record

The official retrospective labels the fourth edition “1998 spring,” gives March 20-22 and 147,913 visitors, and records commemorative gifts for the first groups in the admission queue.[^tgs-history] This is event-wide admission context, not a record of kigurumi participation or etiquette rules.

### October 9-11: completed TGS 1998 autumn record

The same source labels the fifth edition “1998 autumn” and gives October 9-11 and 156,455 visitors.[^tgs-history] Total attendance does not establish the scale, first appearance, or participation of any performance-costume category.

### Later specialist publishing and rules remain in their source years

The [2003 volume](../2003/index.md) retains Inside Doll's public starting point, while the [2004 volume](../2004/index.md) retains the earliest currently verifiable public rules for large kigurumi. Neither is moved into 1998, and unlabeled people in official history images are not assigned an identity.
