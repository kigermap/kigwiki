# 2021 Kigurumi Chronicle

> This volume records events, works and publications from this year. Announcements and completed events are identified separately.

## Verified chronology

### May 22 and August 7–8: WCS adopts a hybrid format

The WCS 2021 archive announced its hybrid structure on May 22 and held the event on August 7–8. The championship used a Video Division, while in-person cosplay activity remained around Oasis 21 and other Nagoya venues.[^wcs2021] Japan National Tourism Organization and Japan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs also confirm the dates and hybrid format.[^jnto][^mofa]

### October 23–24: 18th `Kigurumi Wasshoi!`

The organizer records that the event moved from September to October 23–24 and was tested at Hotel East 21 Tokyo.[^wasshoi18] The program combined:

- two days of halls, a top-floor lounge, pool areas, and chapel photography;
- hotel packages that allowed changing in guest rooms;
- stage and dealer activity related to kigurumi and other full-head costumes;
- a dinner plan adjusted to soft drinks under the dining restrictions then in force.

### August 30: the official WCS booklet is archived

WCS published its 2021 official booklet after the event.[^wcs2021] Programs, maps, rules, and booklets are first-party records even when they appear after an event ends.
