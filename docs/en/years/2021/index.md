# 2021 Kigurumi Chronicle

> The repository already contains completed 2022–2025 volumes. Following the rule to avoid completed years and move backward one year at a time, this edition covers 2021. Gaps are preserved instead of being filled with inference.

![2021 public-record timeline](../../../assets/images/kigurumi-2021-chronicle/timeline_2021.svg)

## 1. Scope

This page covers animegao kigurumi, masked character cosplay, specialist events, and the public infrastructure they directly depend on. World Cosplay Summit (WCS) is an adjacent general-cosplay venue: it documents how public events restarted, but it is not evidence that every participant was a kigurumi performer.

Organizer pages and government or public-agency records can establish dates, places, and event structure. Participant media and search summaries are research leads only. Surviving event pages without a verifiable date are not placed on the 2021 timeline.

## 2. Verified chronology

### May 22 and August 7–8: WCS adopts a hybrid format

The WCS 2021 archive announced its hybrid structure on May 22 and held the event on August 7–8. The championship used a Video Division, while in-person cosplay activity remained around Oasis 21 and other Nagoya venues.[^wcs2021] Japan National Tourism Organization and Japan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs also confirm the dates and hybrid format.[^jnto][^mofa]

For this archive, WCS is relevant as event infrastructure: masked cosplay requires changing, transport, assistance, photography, and rest arrangements. The 2021 record is a transitional model; the broader in-person restart belongs in the 2022 volume.

### October 23–24: 18th `Kigurumi Wasshoi!`

The organizer records that the event moved from September to October 23–24 and was tested at Hotel East 21 Tokyo.[^wasshoi18] The program combined:

- two days of halls, a top-floor lounge, pool areas, and chapel photography;
- hotel packages that allowed changing in guest rooms;
- stage and dealer activity related to kigurumi and other full-head costumes;
- a dinner plan adjusted to soft drinks under the dining restrictions then in force.

This is not a duplicate of the 19th event documented in 2022. The two-day hotel model is the direct predecessor to that later record.

### August 30: the official WCS booklet is archived

WCS published its 2021 official booklet after the event.[^wcs2021] Programs, maps, rules, and booklets are first-party records even when they appear after an event ends.

## 5. De-duplication and conclusion

This page retains only the first WCS hybrid model and the 18th `Kigurumi Wasshoi!`. FGO greetings, `KiguFesta`, DW5–12, later WCS rules, and other 2022–2025 material stay in their completed volumes. The strongest 2021 conclusion is modest: general and specialist events found different ways to resume, while undated archive leads still require primary evidence.
