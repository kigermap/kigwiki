# 2001 Kigurumi Chronicle

> The 2002-2025 volumes are complete. Following the rule to avoid completed years and move backward by only one year per run, this volume adds 2001 only. Tokyo Game Show's official thirtieth-anniversary page establishes the dates and attendance of its spring and autumn 2001 events, but it does not label any photograph, program, or statistical field as kigurumi / animegao. This volume records that neighboring public context and evidence boundary instead of backfilling it from 2002-2004.

## 1. Evidence boundary

Tokyo Game Show's official history lists the spring 2001 event as its tenth edition, held March 30-April 1 with 118,080 visitors. It lists the autumn event as the eleventh edition, held October 12-14 with 129,626 visitors. The spring entry also records a Bill Gates keynote.[^tgs-history] These are organizer retrospective records and therefore establish event completion more directly than a planned calendar.

The same history page supplies several photographs and a poster for each edition, but does not provide item-level captions identifying people, costume categories, or kigurumi / animegao. Attendance statistics and uncatalogued photographs cannot independently establish specialist rules, community participation, or a specific performer. The page also gives no explicit license for this site's republication of the images, so this volume links the source without copying its media.

Inside Doll's first-party homepage places that specialist site's start in April 2003. Its update log also places formal publication in 2003, although its exact date conflicts with the homepage.[^inside-main][^inside-news] Those records prevent backdating that site to 2001; they do not prove that no other kigurumi practice, personal page, or event existed that year.

## 2. Verifiable nodes

### March 30-April 1: TGS 2001 Spring completion record

The official thirtieth-anniversary retrospective labels the tenth edition “2001 Spring,” gives March 30-April 1 and 118,080 visitors, and records the Bill Gates keynote.[^tgs-history] This is neighboring public-convention context, not a kigurumi participation record.

### October 12-14: TGS 2001 Autumn completion record

The same official page labels the eleventh edition “2001 Autumn” and gives October 12-14 and 129,626 visitors.[^tgs-history] It supplies no directly citable kigurumi, animegao, large-kigurumi, changing, cloakroom, or photography rule, so total visitors are not converted into related participation.

### Later specialist publishing and rules are not moved backward

The [2003 volume](../2003/index.md) retains Inside Doll's public start, while the [2004 volume](../2004/index.md) retains the earliest directly verifiable public large-kigurumi rules. Neither is moved into 2001, and unidentified subjects in official history photographs are not assigned to a specialist costume category.

## 5. Annual conclusion

The stable 2001 record is limited to actual dates and organizer retrospective attendance for Tokyo Game Show's spring and autumn editions, plus the boundary that these materials do not establish specialist kigurumi / animegao rules or participation. The 2003 specialist site and 2004 public rules remain in their source years. The next unfinished year is 2000.
