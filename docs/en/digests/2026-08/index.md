### August 21-23: DW14 entered its final day; public evidence remains bounded to day one

Doll Weekend's official page continues to list DW14 for August 21-23, 2026 in Huizhou, Guangdong, under the theme “Dreaming Back to Lingnan.” At the August 23 review, it still displayed ticket links and marked recruitment as active.[^dw14] After the event entered its date window, 五代雄介-初音未来kiger, 望月_kiger, and 繁星游弋似歌 posted “DWday1 completed successfully,” “DW14 Day1 Part1 / Part2,” and “DW14 first day: exhausted.” 老骚豆豉 also published “DW14-20260821 Huizhou doll exhibition personal livestream recording D1”; public metadata dates it to August 22 at 01:27 (Asia/Shanghai) and gives a duration of 3 hours, 0 minutes, 52 seconds.[^dw14-day1]

The four independent publishers and explicit Day1 / first-day labels corroborate that **public participation records exist for day one**. The long-form recording adds density without changing the evidence boundary. This is an implementation-status update to July's announcement, not a duplicate event. The sources do not establish the full program, attendance, or completion of all three days, and the official page has not changed to “ended.” The digest therefore does not infer scale, participant identities, or unpublished arrangements; a later organizer retrospective or post-event source can extend the same event chain.

### August 14: HaiDolls published a kigurumi finished work showcase

Bilibili's public metadata dates “kigurumi finished work showcase” to August 14, 2026 at 12:00 (Asia/Shanghai), names HaiDolls as the publisher, and gives a duration of 16 seconds.[^bili-finished] No matching BV identifier or title was found in the site, so it is included as a new monthly **finished-work showcase / daily publication**.

The public description is empty, and the metadata does not identify the character, mask materials, workshop process, or commission relationship. The digest therefore does not expand “finished work showcase” into a tutorial, review, or workshop profile. One short video does not justify a separate workshop section; later process or materials documentation can extend the same source chain.

WCS's official page and structured event data continue to list its Nagoya program for July 31 through August 2, 2026, with `endDate` set to August 2 and status still marked `EventScheduled`.[^wcs] The August 23 review found no updated status field or organizer summary, so only the official calendar window can be said to have elapsed.
