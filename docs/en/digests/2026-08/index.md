### August 21-24: DW14 records expand from day one to three days and post-event retrospectives

Doll Weekend's official page continues to list DW14 for August 21-23, 2026 in Huizhou, Guangdong, under the theme “Dreaming Back to Lingnan.” At the August 24 review, it still displayed ticket links and marked recruitment as active, so the page had not received a post-event status update.[^dw14] The four first-day clips and long-form recording obtained before August 23 continue to establish public participation records for Day1.[^dw14-day1]

On August 24, 一蓑烟雨f published “DW14 Day1 retrospective,” “DW14 Day2,” and “DW14 Day3” from one account. All three public metadata records carry `kigurumi` and `Doll Weekend 14` tags, and the Day3 description says DW14 had ended. Yo桜の御離 separately published “Like a dream: until next time, DW14.”[^dw14-three-days] The three-day sequence plus an independent post-event retrospective updates the evidence from “day one only” to “public participant records span all three dates.” These remain participant-generated records: they do not establish the organizer's full program, exact attendance, or formal closeout, and the digest does not infer scale, identities, or unpublished arrangements.

### August 23: a blinking head shell receives a wearing and internal-detail showcase

Bilibili's public metadata dates “Wearing a blinking head shell + internal-detail showcase” to August 23, 2026 at 17:02 (Asia/Shanghai), names 真爱你的辣条 as publisher, gives a four-minute duration, and includes `kigurumi` and `head shell` tags.[^bili-blink] The title and tags support classification as a monthly workshop / craft and educational lead. It also supplies a wearing and internal-structure angle absent from the August 14 finished-work clip.

The public description is only a hyphen. It does not identify materials, eyelid drive, controls, maker, or whether the structure is original. The digest therefore records a new technical showcase, not a tutorial, product review, or technical first. Blinking head shells are not presented as a concept first appearing this month.

### August 14: HaiDolls published a kigurumi finished work showcase

Bilibili's public metadata dates “kigurumi finished work showcase” to August 14, 2026 at 12:00 (Asia/Shanghai), names HaiDolls as the publisher, and gives a duration of 16 seconds.[^bili-finished] No matching BV identifier or title was found in the site, so it is included as a new monthly **finished-work showcase / daily publication**.

The public description is empty, and the metadata does not identify the character, mask materials, workshop process, or commission relationship. The digest therefore does not expand “finished work showcase” into a tutorial, review, or workshop profile. One short video does not justify a separate workshop section; later process or materials documentation can extend the same source chain.

### WCS 2026: official date window elapsed; actual completion remains pending

WCS's official page and structured event data continue to list its Nagoya program for July 31 through August 2, 2026, with `endDate` set to August 2 and status still marked `EventScheduled`.[^wcs] The August 24 review found no updated status field or organizer summary, so only the official calendar window can be said to have elapsed. This is a **status correction**, not a second event entry, and the date alone does not prove that every program took place.

No organizer summary or independent retrospective sufficient to establish completion was found by the research cutoff, so the event is not described as completed. Full-face costume, photography-consent, and heat-risk rules were already documented in the [July 2026 digest](../2026-07/index.md) and are not repeated here.
